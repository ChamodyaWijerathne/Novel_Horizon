Welcome to Novel Horizon

At Novel Horizon, we are dedicated to introducing readers around the world to the captivating stories of South Asia. Our platform specializes in providing translated South Asian novels, making it easier for readers to explore the rich literary traditions of the region, from heartwarming tales to epic adventures.


Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge cultural gaps through storytelling. By offering high-quality translations of South Asian novels, we aim to make these unique stories accessible to a global audience. We are committed to sharing the diverse voices, histories, and imaginations of South Asia with readers everywhere

Our Vision

We believe that stories have the power to unite people across borders. Our vision is to become the leading platform for translated South Asian novels, helping readers discover stories that may have been previously inaccessible due to language barriers. Novel Horizon is committed to promoting the works of talented authors from South Asia and preserving the essence of their stories in translation.

Get Involved

We’re always looking for feedback from our readers and contributions from translators and authors. Whether you're a passionate reader, a translator, or an author, we would love to hear from you. Help us grow our library and continue sharing the beauty of South Asian literature with the world.

Thank you for choosing Novel Horizon. Embark on a journey into the world of South Asian storytelling and experience the magic of translated novels today.
