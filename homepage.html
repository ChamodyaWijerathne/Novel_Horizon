Coming Soon: "The Legendary Moonlight Sculptor"

We're happy to announce that the beloved Korean web novel The Legendary Moonlight Sculptor, a story about Lee Hyun and his adventures in the virtual reality game Royal Road, will soon be available in more languages on our platform! A must-read for fans of MMORPGs and adventure novels.

Fans of Chinese martial arts and cultivation novels are in for a treat! We're bringing Martial Peak to our platform. This novel follows Yang Kai's relentless pursuit to reach the pinnacle of the martial arts world. Prepare for a thrilling journey of perseverance and strength.
